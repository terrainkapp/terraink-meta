### 4. Map Rendering (OpenFreeMap)
To display maps, the app loads vector map tiles from **OpenFreeMap** ([https://openfreemap.org](https://openfreemap.org)). The underlying map data originates from OpenStreetMap and its contributors.

When the map loads, the app establishes a direct connection to OpenFreeMap's servers. Your IP address and the requested map sections are transmitted in the process. Without this transmission, the map – the core function of the app – cannot be displayed.

**Purpose:** Displaying the interactive map.<br>
**Legal Basis:** Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR – legitimate interest in providing the core function of the app.

### 5. Location Search and Address Resolution (Nominatim / OpenStreetMap)
For location search and for converting coordinates into addresses (reverse geocoding), we use **Nominatim**, a service of the OpenStreetMap Foundation, St John's Innovation Centre, Cowley Road, Cambridge, CB4 0WS, United Kingdom.

When you use the search function, your search term and IP address are transmitted to Nominatim. When you use the location feature (see Section 6), **your location coordinates** are additionally transmitted to Nominatim in order to determine a place name.

**Purpose:** Location search and resolution of place names.<br>
**Legal Basis:** Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR – legitimate interest in providing the search function; where location coordinates are transmitted, additionally Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR – consent, given via your device's location permission.

Privacy policy: [https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy)

### 6. Location Detection
The app offers a feature that displays your current location on the map. This feature runs **only when you actively trigger it** by tapping "Get my location". The first time, your device asks whether you allow the app to access your location.

Location detection is performed via your device's location services. We do not store your location data on our own servers. However, in order to determine the corresponding place name, your coordinates are transmitted to Nominatim (see Section 5).

You can revoke the location permission at any time in your device settings: on iOS via **Settings → Terraink → Location**, on Android via **Settings → Apps → Terraink → Permissions**.

**Legal Basis:** Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR – consent – and § 25 (1) TDDDG.

### 7. Local Storage on the Device
So that your work is not lost and the interface remains convenient, the app stores certain information locally on your device, including:

- placed markers and map content
- recently used locations
- counters and interface state (e.g. notices already shown)
- acknowledgement of legal documents you have reviewed

This data remains **on your device** and is not transmitted to us or to third parties. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or, on Android, clear the app data in the settings.

**Legal Basis:** § 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG – strictly necessary to provide the service you have expressly requested – in conjunction with Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.
